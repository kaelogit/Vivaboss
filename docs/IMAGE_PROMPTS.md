# Vivaboss Fusion — image generation scripts

Brand look for every prompt: warm craft × modern UK service. Palette: deep charcoal `#121110`, warm paper `#F9F8F6`, copper accent `#B4532A`. Natural light, documentary / editorial photography, **no text overlays**, no logos, no watermarks, no purple neon, no generic stock-smile portraits.

**Negative (append to every tool that supports it):**  
`blurry, low quality, watermark, text, logo, brand name, purple lighting, neon, cartoon, illustration, plastic skin, crowded collage, UI mockup`

Use Midjourney, ChatGPT Images, Flux, or Ideogram. Aspect ratios noted per shot.

**Why the first versions failed:** packing craft + door + parcel into one frame usually makes a busy “stock collage.” Heroes need **one dominant beautiful subject**, huge negative space for the word VIVABOSS, and only *hints* of the other arms.

Mood: cinematic, luxurious, calm, tactile, independent UK lifestyle brand. Colour grade: deep charcoal #121110 shadows, warm paper #F9F8F6 highlights, natural leather browns, one restrained copper specular highlight. Photorealistic editorial photography.
